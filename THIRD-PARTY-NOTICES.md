# Third-Party Notices

The StarBridge desktop client uses third-party NuGet packages. Their package
metadata and license notices are available through NuGet and in the respective
upstream projects:

- Microsoft.Web.WebView2
- Vortice.Direct3D11
- Vortice.DirectComposition

The projects also target .NET 8 and are subject to the licenses and notices
distributed with the selected .NET runtime and SDK.

Star Citizen, Squadron 42, Roberts Space Industries, Cloud Imperium and related
names and marks belong to their respective owners. No third-party game media is
included in this source export. Ship screenshots, ship detail artwork and system
map images used by internal builds are deliberately excluded because their
redistribution basis has not been established for the Apache-2.0 source package.

The source package's remaining bitmap files are limited to StarBridge brand
artwork and project UI artwork. Their treatment is documented in
`ASSET_POLICY.md`; they are not Star Citizen screenshots or extracted game
media.
